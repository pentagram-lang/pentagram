# Tests

## Combine quality evidence

**Task**

A recovery guide passes every documentation lint. In a documentation reader trial, the reader follows the guide but takes the wrong recovery action. An independent review establishes that the necessary warning is accurate but buried after the action it governs.

A second recovery guide also passes every documentation lint. No reader trial or review has been run, even though the consequence of a misunderstanding is high and reader use remains uncertain.

Explain the role of criteria, lint, test, and review. Give the quality judgement supported for each guide, explain why, and name the governing sources you used.

**Assert**

- Criteria supplies the applicable requirements and governs the final quality judgement.
- Lint, test, and review are treated as bounded evidence rather than competing authorities or independent approvals.
- The first guide fails because available evidence establishes an applicable documentation defect.
- The lint pass does not compensate for the failed reader outcome or accepted review finding.
- The second guide is inconclusive because the lint result does not address the material reader uncertainty.
- The answer does not prescribe one universal order for gathering lint, test, and review evidence.
- The answer cites the quality and criteria contracts and explains how they govern the judgements.
